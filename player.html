🏀 Coach Ranking View — Instructions

What this page is for. Every player is listed here so you can rank them and leave notes ahead of the draft (or the mid-season re-rank). Your seeds combine with every other coach's into one Composite Seed, which is what drives the draft board.

Seed (1–8). Tap a number to rate how you'd rank that player relative to the whole player pool — 1 is your top pick, 8 is your lowest. Every player should eventually get a seed from you.

Modifier (Strong / Mid / Low). A finer read within the same seed number — e.g. two players might both be a "4" to you, but one is a Strong 4 (closer to a 3) and the other a Low 4 (closer to a 5). Optional, but it helps break ties in the composite.

Notes. Free text, visible to every coach. Use it for what you saw at tryouts or in games — strengths, concerns, position fit. Keep it respectful; these are public to the whole coaching group.

Composite Seed. The average of every coach's submitted seed for that player. Tap it to see the individual breakdown. If a single ballot looks off to you, flag it to the commissioner rather than adjusting your own seed to compensate — see below.

Sort & filter. The bar above the list is the same one on the Player Directory. Sort by Team to see your own roster grouped together, or by Has Video to review only players with tryout footage. Filter chips (Grade, Team, Favorites, etc.) narrow the list; the search box matches by name.

Favorites. Tap the heart on a player's photo to bookmark them — useful for shortlisting players you want to revisit, independent of your seed for them.

Rank honestly, as an assessment of the player — not as a draft-order wishlist. The composite only means what it's supposed to if everyone's numbers reflect ability.
